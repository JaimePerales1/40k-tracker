Keyword Codex

Quick, in-your-own-words reminders of common core rule keywords — handy for jogging your memory mid-game. These are simplified summaries, not official rules text. Always defer to your rulebook, the official app, or your group's ruling for exact wording and current errata.

Attacks
Anti-<Keyword> X+
Against units with the matching keyword (like Infantry, Vehicle, or Monster), any wound roll of X or higher automatically counts as a critical wound — even if it normally wouldn't have been one. Great for cutting through specific target types.
Lethal Hits
A critical hit (an unmodified roll of 6 to hit) automatically wounds the target — no wound roll needed for that attack.
Devastating Wounds
A critical wound (an unmodified roll of 6 to wound) turns into mortal wounds equal to the weapon's damage, and the target doesn't get to make a save against it.
Sustained Hits X
A critical hit generates X extra hits on top of the one you already scored — more shots for free when you roll well.
Twin-linked
You get to re-roll wound rolls for this weapon. Simple and always useful.
Precision
Hits from this weapon can be allocated to an enemy Character model in the target unit, even if that model isn't normally the closest or otherwise eligible to be picked.
Heavy
The weapon gets +1 to hit if the model firing it didn't move this turn (stayed stationary).
Assault
This weapon can still be fired even if the unit Advanced this turn (normally Advancing stops you shooting).
Rapid Fire X
If the target is within half the weapon's range, add X extra attacks with this weapon.
Pistol
Can be fired even while your unit is within engagement range of the enemy (normally not allowed), and can target the unit you're locked in combat with.
Blast
This weapon gets extra attacks against bigger units — roughly +1 attack for every 5 models in the target unit. Rewards shooting into big blobs.
Ignores Cover
The target doesn't get any benefit from being in cover against this attack.
Indirect Fire
This weapon can target units it can't draw line of sight to. It takes a penalty to hit, and the target still benefits from cover (and gets extra benefit if entirely out of sight).
Hazardous
Risky to use — after firing or fighting with it, you roll a die for the model; on a bad result, that model takes damage or is destroyed. A trade-off for extra power.
Torrent
This weapon automatically hits its target — no roll to hit required.
Melta X
If the target is within half the weapon's range, add X to this weapon's Damage characteristic for that attack.
Firing Deck X
Up to X models embarked inside this transport can shoot their weapons out of it, as if they were still on the battlefield.
One Shot
This weapon can only be fired once per game — after it's used, it's spent for the rest of the match.
Extra Attacks
Attacks made with this weapon are made in addition to the model's normal attacks — it doesn't replace anything, it just adds more.
Fights First
This unit fights in the Fight phase before any "normal" units get to, regardless of charge order — a big advantage in melee.
Movement & Deployment
Deep Strike
Instead of setting up on the battlefield at the start of the game, this unit can arrive later, appearing anywhere more than 9" away from enemy models.
Infiltrators
During deployment, this unit can be set up anywhere on the battlefield as long as it's more than 9" from both the enemy deployment zone and any enemy models — letting it start much further forward than usual.
Scouts X
After both sides have finished deploying but before the game begins, this unit can make a free move of up to X inches, as long as it stays away from the enemy.
Durability
Feel No Pain X
Each time this model would lose a wound, roll a die first — on a roll of X or higher, ignore that wound entirely. A extra layer of protection on top of saves.
Stealth
Attacks targeting this unit take a -1 penalty to hit, making the unit harder to shoot or fight effectively.
Other
Leader
This Character model can attach to a compatible unit (its "bodyguard") and fight/move as part of that unit instead of on its own.
Support
Works a lot like Leader — during army muster, before the battle starts, a Support unit attaches to a friendly bodyguard unit and stays with it for the whole game. The difference: a bodyguard unit can have both a Leader and a Support unit attached at the same time, unless something says otherwise.
